## Brand & Style

This design system embodies defense-grade analytics, enterprise risk surveillance, and state-backed institutional authority tailored for JAGA (Jaringan Analitik Guard Anti-fraud) within the JKN healthcare infrastructure. The aesthetic fuses the deep, focused telemetry of Palantir-style operational interfaces with the clarity and executive legibility of high-end analytical SaaS.

The personality is authoritative, precise, forensic, and uncompromised. The visual language conveys:
- **Absolute Precision**: Exact mathematical alignments, microscopic telemetry, clean structural grid lines, and high-density information architecture.
- **Deep Signal Clarity**: An intense navy substrate counterbalanced by sterile analytical white workspaces, allowing critical anomalies and risk alerts to emerge with undeniable visual hierarchy.
- **Institutional Weight**: A balance of sovereign credibility and cutting-edge artificial intelligence, projecting vigilance, trust, and proactive defense against institutional fraud.

## Colors

The palette uses a deliberate dual-core operational model: a dominant, high-focus dark telemetry base for tactical screens and network graphs, paired with structured analytical paper-white zones for dense audits and legal reporting.

- **Primary (`#35F2A0` / `#8BFFCE`)**: Vibrant Signal Green. Signifies verified integrity, active AI models, baseline health, and positive telemetry matches.
- **Secondary (`#0D1130` / `#131735`)**: Deep Navy & Tactical Slate. Forms the operational canvas, side rails, containment headers, and elevated panel layers.
- **Alert & Risk Accents**:
  - Critical Fraud Alert: `#FF5C67` (immediate intervention required, high-probability collusion).
  - High Risk: `#FF9F43` (anomalous billing patterns, cluster spikes).
  - Medium Risk / Warning: `#FFD166` (outlier provider metrics, pending verification).
  - Analytical Neutral Light: `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` for executive export views, audit records, and light diagnostic cards.
  - Structural Hairlines: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark surfaces; `rgba(13, 17, 48, 0.08)` on analytical white surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a tri-font framework built for maximum tactical utility:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, institutional gravitas with subtle geometric warmth, anchoring high-level risk dashboards and section demarcations.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Provides pristine, high-density legibility across forensic investigations, claim descriptions, and multi-tier metadata.
- **Telemetry & Labels (JetBrains Mono)**: Encodes raw system metrics, case identifiers (e.g., `JKN-RISK-8802`), geospatial coordinates, confidence ratings, and anomaly vector values.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column modular fluid grid anchored by compact, high-density spatial units. Spacing follows an absolute 4px/8px modular base designed for multi-pane command environments.

- **Desktop (1440px+)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and 32px margins. Multi-pane panels support simultaneous inspection of graph networks, telemetry sidebars, and active tabular ledgers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Graph visualization collapsible into tabbed inspection drawers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Telemetry tiles collapse to full-width card stacks with sticky risk indicators.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, but on **tonal layered surfaces and razor-sharp ghost hairlines**.

- **Base Layer (`#080B24`)**: Infinite deep floor used for network topology canvasses and global root layouts.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0D1130`)**: Primary structural panels, navigation rails, and analytical card containers bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#131735`)**: Hovered table rows, popover telemetry inspector cards, flyout nodes, and contextual query bars.
- **High-Alert Focus**: Elements bearing critical anomaly scores (`#FF5C67`) receive an ultra-tight luminous aura: `0 0 16px rgba(255, 92, 103, 0.25)` accompanied by a crisp 1px stroke.
- **Light Analytical Canvas (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`)**: Applied to specialized sub-surfaces (e.g., audit dossiers, court-ready fraud exports) bounded by `1px solid rgba(13, 17, 48, 0.08)` to present undeniable document-level contrast.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly calibrated to **Soft (0.25rem / 4px base)** to retain the disciplined, military-grade intelligence look and feel. 

Sharp structural corners are modulated only slightly to prevent visual fatigue while avoiding playful consumer softness:
- Micro badges, status chips, and table pills: `2px - 4px`.
- Operational cards, network inspectors, and telemetry containers: `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
- Modal sheets and floating command bars: `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- Circular nodes (`rounded-full`) are strictly reserved for network graph vertices, AI agent telemetry status dots, and biometric confirmation indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Signal Primary**: `#35F2A0` background, `#080B24` bold typography, `4px` radius. Used exclusively for definitive triage actions: "Freeze Provider Payout", "Authorize Intervention", "Deploy Model".
- **Tactical Secondary**: Transparent navy background with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Critical Action**: `#FF5C67` surface with dark text or 1px ghost border with `#FF5C67` text for high-risk manual overrides.

### Anomaly Chips & Badges
- Constructed with a `1px` stroke, subtle tinted translucent fill (`rgba(r, g, b, 0.12)`), and `JetBrains Mono` label text.
- Formats: `[CRIT 98.4%]` in Signal Red, `[WARN - SPIKE]` in Signal Amber, and `[VERIFIED]` in Signal Green.

### Enterprise Data Tables
- Micro-padded rows (36px compact, 44px standard) separated by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Monospaced numerical values right-aligned with fixed tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Sticky headers on `#0D1130` with subtle upper borders.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#0D1130` inset with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state illuminates a sharp `1px solid #35F2A0` hairline without heavy outer rings.
- Integrated query prefix markers (e.g., `entity:`, `icd-10:`, `nik:`) styled in dimmed mono labels.

### Network Graph Nodes
- Multi-tier relational rings representing healthcare entities (Hospital, Doctor, Patient, Pharmacist).
- Core vertices: 8px to 16px with glowing orbital state rings signaling detected fraud rings, inflated prescription volumes, or identity-borrowing syndicates.
